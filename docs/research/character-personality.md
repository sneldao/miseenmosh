# Character & personality — best-in-class research

How the best games turn systems into people, and what we're stealing for each of the five character beats.

## Sources (Serper, deep)

- **Hades — dialogue as consumable content (Game Design Lab / teemo.dev).** Hades' most-copied idea: dialogue is gated by run state, not fixed to levels. Every death/escape unlocks new eligible lines. Conversations are 2–4 lines (attention budget respected). NPCs react persistently to past actions (memory + continuity). Explicit rule: *no line punishes the player* — failure is met with curiosity, sympathy, or comedy (Hypnos). Domestic register, mythic stakes: gods are relatives with opinions.
- **Hades relationship arcs (datafield.dev case study).** Each NPC has dozens of exchanges forming one arc (Achilles: formal → guarded → confessional → favor). Progression via Nectar/Ambrosia gifts → keepsakes: persistent mechanical tokens of relationships.
- **RimWorld (RPS / Sylvester).** Small casts (not 200 colonists) so the player holds a remembered history of each one. Labels create meaning: "sister" makes an insult land differently. Tiny opinion modifiers from events compound in the player's head into story.
- **Undertale (GamesBeat / GDC / academic).** Bosses spared show up later as NPCs; absence has emotional weight. The world *reacts* without judging. Flowey remembers even across save resets. Sparing requires understanding each monster (bespoke puzzle per character), not a generic button.
- **Supporting:** Supergiant voice-acting as primary characterization; keepsakes/companions as gameplay-valued affection; Orpheus/Eurydice reunion as long-arc payoff.

## What we apply (and where)

1. **101 gets a face.** RimWorld labels + Hades codex: naming the regulars MARA & JUNE turns a seat assignment into a relationship. Venue migration = their arc (corner → window → center stage). House-vow-held → permanent regulars = Hades keepsake: a persistent mechanical token (mercy aura) that says the relationship changed the world.
2. **Cam triangle.** Undertale bespoke reactions + Hades no-punish rule: the same player action (wiring the cam) gets three different receptions. Kid performs (helps + taxes), GILLIAM respects tradecraft (harder to find, better paid), MADAME V approves (warmth, +ego). Curiosity is never punished; it is priced or praised.
3. **BEEFCAKES night.** Undertale spare arc: the joke antagonist gets one night as a real threat with a real choice. Détente → he shows up later as texture (truce barks, quiet street). Rivalry → he escalates (doubled runners). The world reacts; the morning after is different either way.
4. **Cooks remember.** RimWorld event-memory + Hades keepsakes: twice-praised cooks earn a once-a-night save (PETRA catches, DIMITRI pre-whisks, ANWAR stays). Small, announced, permanent-ish. The brigade stops being a roster and starts being *your* brigade.
5. **Endings in their voices.** Undertale's phone-call epilogue: spared characters get the last word. Our review already judges; the epilogue lets each persona speak last in their own register, tiered by stars. The last thing read is a person, not a number.
