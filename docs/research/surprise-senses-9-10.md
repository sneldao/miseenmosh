# Best-in-class: surprise, emotion & the senses (9/10 research)

Date: 20 Nov 2026. Sources: Serper deep + Tavily advanced (`tvly-dev-…Wasw`).

## 1. Systems suspense beats content surprise
Polaris: surprise = understanding changed; suspense = excitedly aware it *could* change.
Elden Ring Siofra Well (minute-long elevator, world-model breaks), BotW chemistry
(metal boomerang + storm + powder kegs = unwritten story). Magic is between A and B,
no quest markers, curiosity-driven. Takeaway: one contract-break per residency creates
"will it happen again?" suspense.

## 2. Mechanical subversion = emotion
FDG Beyond Satisfaction: Celeste wind subverts snappy movement → struggle is the tune.
Getting Over It: same climb/fall loop = dread/excitement. Our sear window (F in the gold)
is already this. Next: one pan that *lies* (sounds done at 70%). The ear learns doubt.

## 3. Delight needs space, must be shareable
Dark Imp: delight = spontaneous grins, "I can't believe you did that", funny voices;
crowding kills it. Legacy games ration the new card; unseen cards pull curiosity forward.
Takeaway: one anecdote per night max. The interrupt lock (sauce/request/folklore/runner
never overlap) is the mechanism. Protect it.

## 4. Sound fills the senses you don't have (transmodality)
A Sound Effect: players get vision + hearing; sound *storytells* touch/smell/taste.
Thermoception = room-tone shift (stuffy → airy). ACM: dynamic layers + spatial muffling
= presence. Spence 2021 / Scent Ninja 2026: even imagined smell boosts immersion
("I just imagined it when cutting fruit"). Sonic Delights CHI'25: syncing vibration +
sound to chewing deepens flavour. Our equivalents:
- taste → sizzle pitch (doneness you salivate to)
- smell → language + hiss ("butter hits the pan")
- touch → bell / rip / shatter; add 101 ceramic rattle past 30s
- wonder = Novelty + Pleasantness + no urgency (EXAG) → 0.5s hush when folklore fires,
  then slam. The gap is appetite.

## 5. Sweet = love, bitter = shame
DIS'21 / Obrist: sweet → positive affect, bitter → negative; chewing depth = story depth.
Chewing Jockey (screaming jellybeans) = funny incongruence. Velasco: tie food to
culture/status/emotion, make players active. Stardew/BotW: food = holidays, gifts,
identity — not HP. Cooking Mama: pressure = presentation shame.
Our verbs already map: SERVE (pride), SEND BACK (shame), SMASH (rage), family meal (love).
Provenance (Lorna, layby, risky fish) turns ingredients into affect:
pristine Lorna + Madame V = strings + nod (sweet); risky fish + Kid = sour hiss + post (bitter).

## 6. Overcooked: interdependence, not chaos
Ghost Town GDC: roles went rote; fix = more actions than players + mid-level layout
changes (tilting ship, quake split, separate trucks). Communication becomes mechanic.
Single-player translation: our crew is brigade + room. Don't stack demands; sequence
them. N9 crescendo: stampede → flash → 101 rescue → runner, 20s apart. Composed,
not piled on. The "oh what? we'll never manage this → we did" arc.

## 7. Memory + anticipation = retention
Emotional-context talk: breaking patterns carelessly turns impact comical (Doom beta felt
wrong). Retention = reflective stories you retell. Review card = memory device:
vow → persona → folklore → stars. Office dread ("She drops forks") pays off 60s later.
Dread → release → anecdote → retelling.
