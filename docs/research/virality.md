# Virality & judge-pattern research — Nov 18, 2026

## Verified virality patterns to build in

1. **Title/verb in one sentence** — A Game About Digging a Hole: solo dev,
   14 days, 1M copies; "I focused on the hook." Test: does the screenshot
   explain it without a caption?
2. **Physics-failure comedy** (REPO ~200K CCU, Peak 2M/9 days): mechanics
   that backfire, recoverable failure, funny silhouettes, mid-scream
   clips. Kitchen chaos is a natural fit: fires, dropped plates, walkouts.
3. **Daily-seed + share artifact** (Wordle lineage, Peak's daily
   mountain): same service for everyone → comparable scores → shareable
   result card → streaks. Cheap: seeded PRNG + localStorage.
4. **Funny-to-watch failure** — design for spectation; the clip IS the
   catastrophe (Baby Steps summit falls).
5. **Hidden second mode** (Sprunki): the secret is the content —
   "there's a critic mode nobody's found."
6. **Readable UI artifact as marketing** — the end-of-run card IS the
   ad. "Tonight's Review" must be designed as a share card from day one.

## What judges demonstrably reward (LD58 / GMTK25 / js13k / Game Off)

- One-sentence novel mechanic wins Innovation; humor is its own axis;
  literal theme-fit pays; browser-playable is near-mandatory for rating
  velocity; depth signals are cheap (difficulty options, secrets,
  speedrun timer, endless mode).
- This jam's criteria (equal weight): creativity/originality, depth,
  fun, performance, visual fidelity. MISE EN MOSH's strongest axes are
  creativity (unserved fantasy), depth (interacting systems) and fun
  (service chaos); performance should be trivially strong; **visual
  fidelity is the risk axis — it must be won with art direction and
  curated gen-assets, not volume.**

## AI-asset reception landscape

- Default player sentiment is hostile (JWE3 pulled its AI portraits;
  ARC Raiders docked for AI voices; Roottrees replaced AI art and won).
- BUT this is an **AI jam** — the sponsors are genAI vendors; judges
  chose to judge it. The winning model is **1001 Nights**: AI as the
  mechanic/subject + undeniable craft + principled disclosure.
- Rules of thumb: bake + hand-curate everything; pass the "twisted
  fingers" test (judges hunt artifacts); the generation pipeline should
  be *visible in the game's DNA* (critic detection = judging generated
  tells, voice IS the fantasy).
- Disclose models at publish time (the jam asks for them explicitly).

## Topical timing rules (verified)

- Breaking memes die in 24–72h (Coldplay Canoodlers shipped in a day,
  still "too slow"). Our hooks are all weeks-to-months scale — safe.
- Predictable events > breaking news (Fantapapa model: pre-build, launch
  into the moment — e.g., Kitchen Nightmares Jan 2027 lands *during*
  judging).
- Satirize evergreen targets, not specific people — archetype rival
  chefs, not "Gordon Ramsay." Beefcakes is a character, not a person.

## Screenshot test checklist (for MISE EN MOSH)

- 3-second legibility: screaming chef + ticket storm = instant read
- implied catastrophe mid-frame: pan mid-air, critic mid-bite of a
  burnt dish
- scale contrast: one chef vs a rail of 14 tickets
- the share card as the artifact: review grid + quotable scream
