## The white space (the reason this game should exist)

Nobody has shipped **"be the rockstar chef."** Every cooking game casts
you as anonymous cook #3 or a neutral owner. The TV fantasy is hierarchy:
ONE figure at the pass while a brigade says "yes, chef." Specific gaps:

- ego/reputation/feuds as mechanics
- asymmetric brigade authority (you judge, they cook)
- Kitchen-Nightmares fix-a-failing-restaurant fantasy
- critic/inspector cat-and-mouse (Michelin mystery diners)
- Black-Spoon→White-Spoon underdog progression
- fine-dining satire as mechanics (one scallop, giant plate)

## Durable vs stale

- Durable: Rockstar Chef discourse (airing NOW), Ramsay cycle, Kitchen
  Nightmares Jan, CCW S3 into 2027, "yes chef"/"idiot sandwich"/"where's
  the lamb sauce" vocab, friendslop wave, holiday-service season.
- Stale: The Bear plot discourse (resolved June — use its vocabulary
  only), chef's-table luxury worship ("post your rent" effect — satirize
  luxury, don't celebrate it), micro flavor trends, Cooking Simulator-
  realism (CS2 launched at 49% — fidelity is not the reward).
